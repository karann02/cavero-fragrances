info Logo Guidelines

Following these specs is mandatory for the best visibility and a crisp logo on all screens (including high-resolution / retina displays).

  • aspect_ratio
    Recommended size: {{ REC_WIDTH }} × {{ REC_HEIGHT }} px A horizontal wordmark works best (about a 3:1 width-to-height ratio).
  • hd
    Resolution: upload at 2× size Export at double the display size so the logo stays sharp on retina screens.
  • image
    Format: PNG or SVG (transparent background) Transparency keeps the logo clean over the cream header. JPG/WEBP are also accepted.
  • data_usage
    File size: under {{ MAX_MB }} MB Smaller files load faster and keep the site snappy.
  • crop_free
    Keep a little padding Leave small clear space around the mark so it isn't cropped at the edges.

tips_and_updates Tip: a light or white-edged logo reads best on the dark header; a dark logo on the light cream header. Test it on the live site after saving.
